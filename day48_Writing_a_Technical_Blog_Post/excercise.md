# Exercises – Day 48

1. **Write a first draft**  
   Use the template above to write a 500‑word draft about your capstone project. Include at least one code snippet and one screenshot (or placeholder).

2. **Peer review**  
   Ask a classmate or an AI (using the `improve_writing` function) to review your draft. Incorporate at least 3 pieces of feedback.

3. **Add a demo GIF**  
   Record a short (30‑second) screen recording of your app. Convert to a GIF (use tools like `ffmpeg` or online converters). Embed it in your post.

4. **Publish**  
   Post your blog on a public platform (Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode, or LinkedIn). Share the link in your project’s README.

5. **Bonus: SEO optimisation**  
   Research 3–5 keywords related to your project (e.g., “RAG chatbot tutorial”). Include them naturally in the title and headings.
