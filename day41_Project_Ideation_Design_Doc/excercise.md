# Exercises – Day 41

1. **Brainstorm 5 ideas**  
   Use the LLM helper or your own creativity. Write each idea as a short paragraph.

2. **Select one project**  
   Score each idea on feasibility (1–5), impact (1–5), and personal interest (1–5). Pick the highest total.

3. **Write the design doc**  
   Fill in all sections of `DESIGN.md` with at least 200 words total. Be specific about the tech stack.

4. **Peer review**  
   Exchange design docs with a classmate or use an AI to critique your doc. Ask: “Is the scope achievable in 9 days?”. Adjust if needed.

5. **Bonus: Risk assessment**  
   Identify 3 technical risks (e.g., “model too large to run locally”) and propose mitigations.
